Rates: European Central Bank euro reference rates (via Frankfurter), converted to US dollar pairs; other currencies from exchange-api (public domain).
Rates are checked every 10 minutes but change once per business day: the ECB sets them around 14:15 Frankfurt time. They are reference rates, not live market prices.
