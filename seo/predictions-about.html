About this prediction market heatmap

heat.sala.company/predictions puts the most traded questions on Polymarket, Kalshi, PredictIt, Manifold and Betfair side by side: elections, the economy and the Fed, crypto, sports, technology and world events, with each market's odds and how they moved. Each of the top questions also has a page of its own.

What is a prediction market heatmap?

One view of the most traded questions on several prediction markets. Each tile is one question (the same question on several venues is one tile): its headline and topic icon, the market's answer and its chance, darker and bolder the likelier it is, a chip for how much it moved over a day, a week or a month, and the money bet. The most traded questions get the biggest tiles.

Which prediction markets are included?

Polymarket, Kalshi, PredictIt, Manifold and Betfair, each on its own tab, plus an All tab with the most traded questions across every venue. Manifold uses play money, so its odds aren't backed by real bets. Betfair needs an API key and appears once that is set up.

What does a percentage mean?

The price of a contract that pays 1 if the outcome happens: 62% means traders pay 62 cents for a dollar if it does. It is the market's chance for the outcome, not a forecast by this site.

Why can the same question have different odds on two venues?

The rules for how a question resolves often differ in small ways, and so do fees, who is allowed to trade on each venue and how much money is behind it. A gap between venues is not automatically a free profit.

How often is the data updated?

Every 10 minutes. Nothing here is real-time.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is an experimental, work-in-progress tool. Data can be late, wrong or missing.
